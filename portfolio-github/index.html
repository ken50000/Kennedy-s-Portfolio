Getting paid back meant losing half a shift.

Reimbursements were fragmented across markets, buried in country-specific compliance rules, and running on a system that couldn't scale.

  • Associates used multiple disconnected tools per market
  • Complex tax, audit & reporting requirements
  • No unified platform built to grow with the business

End-to-end UX across three workflows.

I led design across reimbursement, procurement, and cash-advance flows — partnering closely with Product, Engineering, Research, and Finance to validate every decision.

  • Mapped the full journey from receipt to reimbursement
  • Prioritized the guided submission flow first
  • Explored AI-enabled improvements with cross-functional partners

Every choice traded complexity for clarity.

Small interaction decisions that added up to a dramatically faster flow.

  • Guided, step-by-step report creation
  • Smart approval routing for low-risk requests
  • Fully responsive for store & field associates
  • Built-in mileage & route calculator

$21M+ in annual value realized.

Driven by automation, fee avoidance, and reclaimed associate time — with strong usability scores to back it up.

Legacy branding met increasingly complex scheduling.

The portal relied on legacy design standards that couldn't keep pace with modern frameworks — or with planners juggling labor demand, availability, and last-minute changes.

  • Fragmented, inconsistent legacy UI
  • No single source of truth for design + engineering
  • Limited visibility into real-time scheduling gaps

System consistency without losing the edge cases.

I leveraged LD3.5's React-enabled components and PX standards as the foundation, building custom pieces only where scheduling genuinely needed them.

  • Interviewed People Leads managing "borrowed" associates
  • Prioritized the scheduling dashboard as highest-impact
  • Embedded Sidekick AI contextually, not as a bolt-on

An intelligent system, not just a prettier grid.

Every feature was built to reduce the cognitive load of a real-time, high-stakes decision.

  • Centralized dashboard surfacing demand & gaps
  • Real-time adjustments as the day unfolds
  • AI-recommended fixes at the point of conflict

Modernized the platform. Adoption is next.

Feedback has been strong around clarity, consistency, and real-time visibility — with onboarding identified as the next investment.

  • Positive feedback on clarity & consistency
  • Next: contextual tutorials & onboarding flows
  • Next: deeper Sidekick integration

TICKET ANI-2022 · Feb 2022 — Jun 2025 · Nashville, TN

Ani

A 0→1 social platform for animanga fans — built from a blank canvas as founding Product Designer, to 10,000+ downloads in month one.

Role: Founding Product Designer Scope: 0 → 1, full product Focus: Discovery, tracking, community
10K+
Downloads, month one
100K+
Launch video views in 30 days
20+
User interviews in discovery
0→1
Full design system built from scratch

Fans had nowhere built for them.

Existing platforms supported passive content consumption, but lacked meaningful tracking tools or real community-driven interaction.

  • Fragmented discovery across forums & apps
  • Inconsistent, clunky tracking tools
  • Little real community-building functionality

A focused 4-week discovery sprint, first.

Before a single wireframe, I built a deep understanding of user needs from the ground up.

  • 20+ interviews across fan engagement levels
  • Analyzed Reddit, Discord & niche fan communities
  • Competitive analysis via SWOT framework

A system built for speed, then refactored for scale.

Slot-based components let the product iterate fast early on — then evolve into something more durable.

  • Slot-based component patterns for rapid iteration
  • Token-based color system (red primary + 4 secondaries)
  • Type scale built for dense, scannable content

10,000+ downloads validated the bet.

The MVP proved early demand — and showed exactly where to invest next.

  • 10K+ downloads & 100K+ video views, month one
  • Next: deepen personalization & engagement loops
  • Next: strengthen discovery ↔ community connection
