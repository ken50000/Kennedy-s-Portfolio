## Overview

I worked as a UX Designer on a new feature aimed at increasing community engagement and expanding the platform's
challenge pipeline. I designed **Ideas**, a community-driven space where users can submit, discover, and upvote
ideas. The most-supported ideas surface and evolve into official challenges, creating a direct path from
community input to paid opportunities.

## Project goal

Create a community-driven system that turns user-generated ideas into validated challenges, increasing engagement
while expanding Topcoder's opportunity pipeline.

## The problem

Topcoder lacked a structured way for the community to contribute ideas and influence which challenges were
created. Users could take part in existing challenges, but there was no clear path to propose, validate, or
elevate new ideas. That limited engagement and slowed the growth of the challenge pipeline.

## Discovery

Discovery was intentionally lightweight because of team constraints, and because user feedback had already
pointed to a clear opportunity: the community wanted more social interaction, and had no way to contribute beyond
entering challenges. With limited resources, I validated the direction by analyzing existing engagement patterns
and aligning with business goals around participation.

## The approach

I designed a lightweight feedback loop: users submit ideas, others discover and upvote them, and the most relevant
ideas rise naturally. I defined the user flows and interaction patterns for submitting ideas, making them visible,
and moving them into the challenge pipeline. The design balances flexibility for user-generated content with
enough structure to keep ideas consistent and feasible as they become real opportunities.

## An Ideas tab inside the dashboard

<ImageSlot
  folder="topcoder-ideas"

I embedded Ideas directly into the existing dashboard so it felt like part of the workflow, not a separate
product. I added a new navigation entry and a listing that lets users browse, filter by work type, sort by newest
or votes, and switch between open and closed ideas. Upvotes signal demand and inform which ideas should become
challenges. Reusing existing design patterns let me move quickly and focus on the end-to-end experience.

## A submission flow that keeps quality high

<ImageSlot
  folder="topcoder-ideas"
  name="new-idea-modal"
  alt="The New Idea modal: a prompt to review existing ideas first, then step 1 title and category, and step 2 a rich-text description"
  hint="The New Idea modal screen"
  wide
/>

The submission flow balances easy contribution with high-quality, actionable ideas. To cut down on duplicate and
low-value submissions, the modal first asks users to review existing ideas and upvote similar ones. Then two short
steps capture what's needed: a title and work-type category, and a description.

## The result

Ideas added a new layer of community engagement by letting users contribute and validate ideas directly in the
platform. Early internal feedback showed more interaction around idea discovery and voting. Long-term metrics
weren't captured because of team changes.
