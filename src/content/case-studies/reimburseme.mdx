## Context

ReimburseMe is Walmart's expense reimbursement app. Associates use it to submit expenses and get paid back, and
more than 9,000 of them rely on it. I joined the team as a User Interface Designer in July 2024, working with a
senior UX designer, product managers, and engineers.
