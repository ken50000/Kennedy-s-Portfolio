## Overview

Staying active can feel overwhelming. What if fitness felt like a game? Fitness Bingo turns workouts into bingo
challenges. I designed it on my own for a graphic design class.

## The user need

People want a fun way to stay motivated and reach their fitness goals. In Fitness Bingo, they complete exercises
and wellness activities to hit bingo milestones, track their progress, and share wins with friends.

## Problem statement

Fitness fans want a motivating way to try new exercises and track their goals, because the same routine gets
repetitive. A bingo board of varied challenges and milestones keeps things fresh. Sharing achievements and
challenging friends adds motivation and accountability.
