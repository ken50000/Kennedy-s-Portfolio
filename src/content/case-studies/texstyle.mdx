<section class="cine-sec" id="overview">
  <header><span class="cine-num">01</span><h2>Project overview</h2></header>
  <div>
    <p class="cine-lede">TexStyle is a mobile shopping app that shows young shoppers the real cost of their clothes.</p>
    <p>It grades each item on sustainability, labor practices, and transparency, then points people to better alternatives.</p>
    <p>We designed it as a concept for GD468: Interaction Design at Michigan State University, as a team of four.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cine-sec" id="goal">
  <header><span class="cine-num">02</span><h2>The goal</h2></header>
  <div>
    <blockquote class="cine-quote">How might we help young shoppers understand the impact of a piece of clothing, right when they're deciding whether to buy it, without making shopping feel like homework?<cite>Our design question</cite></blockquote>
    <h3>Who we designed for</h3>
    <p>Young, trend-conscious shoppers who buy clothes online often and care about fitting in, but rarely think about where their clothes come from.</p>
    <ol class="cine-flow">
      <li><b>They want</b><span>Stylish clothes at prices they can afford</span></li>
      <li><b>They struggle with</b><span>No quick, trustworthy way to judge a brand's ethics</span></li>
      <li><b>They won't</b><span>Read long reports, or give up style to shop responsibly</span></li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cine-sec" id="challenge">
  <header><span class="cine-num">03</span><h2>The problem</h2></header>
  <div>
    <p class="cine-lede">Young shoppers want trendy clothes, but they have no easy way to see what those clothes really cost the planet and the people who make them.</p>
    <p>Cheap, trend-driven ads push frequent purchases as a way to keep up socially. To meet that demand, fast-fashion stores rely on low-quality materials and unethical labor. The result is clothing waste, pollution, and real harm to garment workers. These costs are hidden at the moment someone decides to buy.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cine-sec" id="strategy">
  <header><span class="cine-num">06</span><h2>Design process</h2></header>
  <div>
    <p>We chose a simple, at-a-glance grade instead of detailed reports, so people can judge an item in seconds.</p>
    <h3>Lo-fi sketches</h3>
    <p>The process started on paper, mapping the full journey from first open to checkout before committing to layouts.</p>
    <ol class="cine-flow cols-4">
      <li><b>Welcome</b><span>A hands-holding-the-globe image sets the app's purpose</span></li>
      <li><b>Style quiz</b><span>“What's your style?” personalizes recommendations</span></li>
      <li><b>Account setup</b><span>Photo, email, username, and password</span></li>
      <li><b>Onboarding</b><span>A carousel introduces the app</span></li>
      <li><b>Home</b><span>Search and clothing categories</span></li>
      <li><b>Category page</b><span>A “?” icon explains each item's sustainability</span></li>
      <li><b>Checkout</b><span>Complete the purchase</span></li>
      <li><b>Reward</b><span>“You win” after purchase, with a prompt to keep browsing</span></li>

<section class="cine-sec" id="solution">
  <header><span class="cine-num">07</span><h2>The solution</h2></header>
  <div>
    <p class="cine-lede">TexStyle turns a hidden problem into a quick, rewarding shopping habit, built around four features.</p>
    <ol class="cine-flow cols-2">
      <li><b>Scan or search</b><span>Scan a barcode in-store or search any item. It meets shoppers where they already are.</span></li>
      <li><b>Sustainability grade</b><span>Scores sustainability, labor practices, and transparency on a visual gauge. Complex ethics, readable in seconds.</span></li>
      <li><b>Better alternatives</b><span>Suggests similar items with stronger grades, so people keep their style without the guilt.</span></li>
      <li><b>Monthly Challenge</b><span>Rewards responsible choices, which keeps people coming back and builds lasting habits.</span></li>
    </ol>
