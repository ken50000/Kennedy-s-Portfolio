<section class="cine-sec" id="goal">
  <header><span class="cine-num">02</span><h2>The goal</h2></header>
  <div>
    <p>Fast fashion drives overconsumption, environmental harm, and unfair labor practices, and shoppers rarely see those costs.</p>
    <p class="cine-lede">Show the hidden costs of a purchase, with a simple grade anyone can read.</p>
    <p>Shoppers search for a brand to see a simple grade for <strong>sustainability</strong>, <strong>labor practices</strong>, and <strong>transparency</strong>, plus more sustainable alternatives.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="cine-sec" id="challenge">
  <header><span class="cine-num">03</span><h2>The challenge</h2></header>
  <div>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Cheap clothing has a cost.</strong> Demand for it leads to low-quality garments, waste, and pollution.</li>
      <li><strong>Ads push frequent buying.</strong> Young people feel pressure to buy often to keep up socially.</li>
      <li><strong>The supply chain is hidden.</strong> Many mass-market stores rely on cheap materials and unethical labor.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>The impact reaches climate change, poverty, and human rights, so it's worth solving.</p>
  </div>
</section>
