## Overview

Texstyle is a shopping app that makes fashion more transparent. It shows shoppers how brands affect people and
the planet, and nudges them toward more responsible choices.

## The user need

Demand for cheap clothing leads to low-quality garments, waste, and pollution. Ads push young people to buy
often to keep up socially, and many mass-market stores rely on cheap materials and unethical labor. The impact
reaches climate change, poverty, and human rights, so it's worth solving.

## Problem statement

Fast fashion drives overconsumption, environmental harm, and unfair labor practices, and shoppers rarely see
those costs. Texstyle makes the hidden costs visible. Shoppers scan a barcode or search for an item to see a
simple grade for **sustainability**, **labor practices**, and **transparency**, plus more sustainable
alternatives.
