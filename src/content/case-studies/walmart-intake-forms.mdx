## Overview

How do you streamline work for one of the world's largest retailers? This project redesigned an internal Walmart
app to make it easier to use, cut wasted steps, and help associates get more done.

## The user need

Third-party vendors and approvers needed an easier, more accessible way to fill out and process intake forms.
Redesigning the app would smooth the process for everyone involved.

## Problem statement

The existing app for submitting intake forms was hard to use and slow for both the people submitting and the
people approving. The process was cumbersome, which hurt productivity and satisfaction.
