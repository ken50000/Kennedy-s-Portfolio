01

Project overview

TexStyle is a mobile shopping app that shows young shoppers the real cost of their clothes.

It grades each item on sustainability, labor practices, and transparency, then points people to better alternatives.

We designed it as a concept for GD468: Interaction Design at Michigan State University, as a team of four.

02

The goal

How might we help young shoppers understand the impact of a piece of clothing, right when they’re deciding whether to buy it, without making shopping feel like homework?Our design question

Who we designed for

Young, trend-conscious shoppers who buy clothes online often and care about fitting in, but rarely think about where their clothes come from.

  1. They wantStylish clothes at prices they can afford
  2. They struggle withNo quick, trustworthy way to judge a brand’s ethics
  3. They won’tRead long reports, or give up style to shop responsibly
03

The problem

Young shoppers want trendy clothes, but they have no easy way to see what those clothes really cost the planet and the people who make them.

Cheap, trend-driven ads push frequent purchases as a way to keep up socially. To meet that demand, fast-fashion stores rely on low-quality materials and unethical labor. The result is clothing waste, pollution, and real harm to garment workers. These costs are hidden at the moment someone decides to buy.

05

Research & testing

Usability testing

I tested TexStyle with fellow students in the fashion major: people who know clothing, materials, and trends better than the average shopper. That made them a tough, credible audience for an app that grades clothing.

06

Design process

We chose a simple, at-a-glance grade instead of detailed reports, so people can judge an item in seconds.

Lo-fi sketches

The process started on paper, mapping the full journey from first open to checkout before committing to layouts.

  1. WelcomeA hands-holding-the-globe image sets the app’s purpose
  2. Style quiz“What’s your style?” personalizes recommendations
  3. Account setupPhoto, email, username, and password
  4. OnboardingA carousel introduces the app
  5. HomeSearch and clothing categories
  6. Category pageA “?” icon explains each item’s sustainability
  7. CheckoutComplete the purchase
  8. Reward“You win” after purchase, with a prompt to keep browsing

Mid-fi wireframes

Next came the sign-in flow in mid-fidelity, to get the structure right. Each screen was paired with a user-action note, so the team could check the flow step by step.

  • Splash screen
  • Registration: continue with Apple, continue with email, or skip
  • Continue with Apple: includes a “Hide my email” option for privacy
  • Continue with email: sign in, terms, and create account
07

The solution

TexStyle turns a hidden problem into a quick, rewarding shopping habit, built around four features.

  1. Scan or searchScan a barcode in-store or search any item. It meets shoppers where they already are.
  2. Sustainability gradeScores sustainability, labor practices, and transparency on a visual gauge. Complex ethics, readable in seconds.
  3. Better alternativesSuggests similar items with stronger grades, so people keep their style without the guilt.
  4. Monthly ChallengeRewards responsible choices, which keeps people coming back and builds lasting habits.
